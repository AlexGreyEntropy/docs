## The parts

| Part | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| Vault | Holds your deposit and your positions |
| Policy | Lists what the vault is allowed to do |
| Worker | Runs your strategy, one per vault |
| Risk engine | Watches every position and acts automatically |
| Pulse Router | Picks the best markets for your vault |

## Worker

Every vault has its own worker. It runs only your strategy and never has access to your wallet's private key.

## Pulse Router

Pulse Router compares the supported markets and picks the best one for each part of the strategy.

| Part | Options |
|------|---------|
| Staking | Nine staking tokens |
| Lending | Kamino, Jupiter Lend |
| Hedge | Pacifica, Phoenix, Bulk (in testing) |

![Supported markets](/images/strategy/venues.jpeg)

## Risk engine

The risk engine follows fixed rules. No one needs to watch a screen.

| When | It |
|------|----|
| Borrow costs spike | Reduces borrowing |
| SOL rises fast | Adds margin to the hedge |
| SOL falls | Takes profit on the hedge and resets it |
| A position drifts | Rebalances it |

## What you sign

You sign to create a vault, to use Yield Upfront, and to close. Everything else runs on its own.
