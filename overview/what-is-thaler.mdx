## The problem

Most yield on Solana only works when SOL's price holds or rises. When SOL falls, your position falls with it, and the yield rarely covers the loss.

## The solution

A Thaler vault is **delta-neutral**. It holds a hedge that cancels out SOL's price moves on your deposit. Your return comes from the yield, not the price.

![Delta-neutral](/images/strategy/delta-neutral.jpeg)

## Where the yield comes from

| Source | Earns most when |
|--------|-----------------|
| Staking | Always |
| Lending loop | Markets are calm or rising |
| Hedge | Markets are volatile or falling |

When one source slows down, another usually picks up.

## Yield in SOL

Your deposit and your yield are counted in SOL. If SOL rises, your SOL is worth more in dollars.

## Your own vault

Deposits are never pooled. Every vault holds only its owner's funds, so a problem in one vault cannot affect another.

## Who it is for

- SOL holders who want more than staking.
- Treasuries and DAOs holding SOL.
- Anyone who does not want to watch charts all day.
