---

## Today

During the private beta, each vault is a **Privy agentic wallet**.

- The vault holds only your funds.
- Thaler runs the strategy with a limited key. It never sees your wallet's private key.
- Payouts can only go to your wallet.

## After the audit

Vaults move to **Squads smart accounts** with policies enforced on chain. The policy is set once when you create the vault and cannot be changed by anyone.

## What the policy controls

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Assets | SOL, supported staking tokens, USDC for hedge profits |
| Markets | Supported staking, lending and perpetual markets only |
| Limits | Set by your risk profile |
| Payouts | Your wallet only |

Anything outside the policy is refused.

## No pooling

Most yield vaults put everyone's funds in one place. Thaler does not. If one vault has a problem, no other vault is affected.
