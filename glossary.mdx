## Vaults

### vault
*(noun)* · *vawlt*

Your own isolated account that holds your deposit and runs one Thaler strategy. No one else's funds share it.

*e.g.: I opened a Balanced **vault** with 10 SOL.*

### risk profile
*(noun)* · *risk · proh · fyle*

The setting you pick when you create a vault, from Safe to YOLO. It decides how much risk the strategy takes and cannot be changed later.

*e.g.: I picked the Safe **risk profile** for my first vault.*

### deposit band
*(noun)* · *dih · poz · it · band*

The minimum and maximum SOL a risk profile accepts.

*e.g.: The **deposit band** for Optimistic starts at 11 SOL.*

### lock period
*(noun)* · *lok · peer · ee · ud*

The first 4 weeks of a vault, when it cannot be closed.

*e.g.: After the **lock period**, I closed my vault and got my SOL back.*

## Yield

### auto-compound
*(noun)* · *aw · toh · kom · pownd*

A vault setting that adds your earnings back in every 4 weeks, so they start earning too. On by default. Switch it off to get paid out instead.

*e.g.: I left **auto-compound** on and let my earnings do the work.*

### auto-claim
*(noun)* · *aw · toh · klaym*

A vault feature that automatically collects earned yield at regular intervals and pays it out to the holder, with no action required.

*e.g.: With **auto-claim** enabled, yield is paid out without manual collection.*

### Yield Upfront
*(noun)* · *yeeld · up · frunt*

A vault option that pays you up to 6 months of expected yield on the day you open the vault.

*e.g.: I used **Yield Upfront** to get four months of yield on day one.*

### auto-repay
*(noun)* · *aw · toh · ree · pay*

The mode a vault runs in after Yield Upfront. Its yield pays back the upfront amount until the vault unlocks.

*e.g.: My vault is on **auto-repay** until the upfront amount is paid back.*

### structural floor
*(noun)* · *struk · cher · ul · flor*

The return the strategy is designed to hold in any market, near 7 to 8 % a year. A target, not a guarantee.

*e.g.: Even in a flat month, the **structural floor** kept my vault earning.*

### service fee
*(noun)* · *sur · vis · fee*

The 11 % of your yield that Thaler keeps. It is never taken from your deposit.

*e.g.: The **service fee** only came out of my yield.*

## Strategy

### delta-neutral
*(adjective)* · *del · tuh · noo · trul*

Hedged so that the price of SOL going up or down does not change the value of your deposit.

*e.g.: My vault is **delta-neutral**, so a SOL drop does not shrink my deposit.*

### liquid staking token
*(noun)* · *lik · wid · stay · king · toh · ken*

A token that stands for staked SOL and keeps earning staking rewards while it is used elsewhere. Also called an LST.

*e.g.: jitoSOL is a **liquid staking token**.*

### lending loop
*(noun)* · *len · ding · loop*

Lending an LST, borrowing SOL against it, and staking that SOL again to earn more.

*e.g.: The **lending loop** kept earning while SOL moved sideways.*

### hedge
*(noun)* · *hej*

A short SOL position that cancels out SOL's price moves on your deposit.

*e.g.: When SOL dropped, the **hedge** turned the move into profit.*

### funding rate
*(noun)* · *fun · ding · rayt*

A payment between traders on a perpetual market. When it is positive, the short side gets paid.

*e.g.: Longs paid the **funding rate** to shorts most of the month.*

### Pulse Router
*(noun)* · *puls · roo · ter*

The part of Thaler that picks the best staking token, lending market and perpetual market for each vault.

*e.g.: **Pulse Router** moved the hedge to a market with better funding.*

## Custody

### agentic wallet
*(noun)* · *ay · jen · tik · wol · it*

The Privy wallet that holds each vault during the private beta. It can only do what the vault's policy allows.

*e.g.: Each beta vault is its own **agentic wallet**.*

### policy
*(noun)* · *pol · ih · see*

The fixed list of actions, assets and markets a vault is allowed to use.

*e.g.: The **policy** only lets my vault use approved markets.*

### smart account
*(noun)* · *smart · uh · kownt*

A Squads account on Solana whose rules are enforced on chain. Vaults move to smart accounts after the audit.

*e.g.: After the audit, my vault becomes a **smart account**.*
