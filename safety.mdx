## Your own vault

Your SOL is never pooled with anyone else's. Every vault holds only its owner's funds. If one vault has a problem, no other vault is affected.

## Who holds your SOL

During the private beta, each vault is a Privy <span className="term term-agentic-wallet" tabIndex={0}>agentic wallet</span>.

- Thaler runs the strategy with a limited key. It cannot see your private key.
- The vault can only use approved tokens and markets.
- Payouts can only go to your Thaler wallet.

## Principal protection

Your principal is protected in dollar value. The <span className="term term-hedge" tabIndex={0}>hedge</span> keeps your deposit's dollar value steady when SOL goes up or down, so you do not lose dollar value on your deposit.

Small gaps can still happen, for example if rates drop or funding turns negative for a while. These are covered by the insurance fund and the treasury. The insurance fund will be made public, with its address, after our funding round.

## Yield floor

Your vault has a guaranteed yield floor: a minimum yield it earns in any market, whether SOL pumps, dumps or does nothing. The floor is set about 3 times lower than what the strategy has earned so far. The lending loop alone earns more than the floor, and funding on top of it roughly doubles it. See [How It Works](/how-it-works#yield-floor).

## Insurance fund and treasury

All of Thaler's revenue goes to two places, each held in its own separate multisig wallet:

| Fund | What it is for |
|------|----------------|
| Treasury | Thaler's revenue from fees. It pays for running the protocol |
| Insurance fund | A backstop for depositors, like insurance on a car or a house. It covers small shortfalls in principal, and is there in case something goes wrong |

The insurance fund is filled from fees, from the extra 1% on [Claim now](/yield#claim-now), and from our funding round. It will be made public, with its address, after the round.

## Rules locked on-chain

Vault actions are checked against fixed policies <span className="term term-on-chain" tabIndex={0}>on-chain</span>. The policies cannot be changed. If a transaction does not pass the policy check, it is rejected automatically, even if someone tries to push a harmful one.

This also means the strategy is rule-based, not run by hand. You can check every action your vault takes on-chain.

## Emergency exit

If a market the vault uses is hacked or fails, an emergency policy unwinds every position and brings the funds back to your vault.

We are adding live monitoring to trigger it, working with Ranger to spot problems at venues and alert the vault fast.

## Staking token depeg

Your vault borrows SOL against a staking token. The vault keeps its loan well below the point where the lending market would sell the collateral. Because of this gap, the staking token can lose about 14 to 15% of its value against SOL before the loan reaches <span className="term term-liquidation" tabIndex={0}>liquidation</span>.

Even past that point, Kamino and Jupiter Lend first sell only part of the collateral to bring the loan back down, instead of closing it all.

A depeg only affects vaults that hold that staking token. If a token your vault does not use loses value, your vault is not affected.

## Audits

Two security firms, [Adevar Labs](https://adevarlabs.com) and [Accretion](https://accretion.xyz), are auditing Thaler in parallel. They cover everything: the on-chain programs, the off-chain worker and backend, the integrations with other apps, and operational security.

We aim to keep auditing on a regular schedule, the way Kamino does.

## If something looks wrong

Safety comes first. If something might be wrong, Thaler pauses vault actions, fixes it and turns them back on. During a pause your positions stay in place and your SOL does not move.

Updates are posted on [@ThalerFinance](https://x.com/ThalerFinance). Report security issues to [support@thaler.finance](mailto:support@thaler.finance).

## Risks that remain

Thaler reduces risk but cannot remove all of it.

| Risk | What could happen |
|------|-------------------|
| Other apps | Kamino, Jupiter Lend, a perpetual market or a staking provider is hacked or fails |
| Staking tokens | A staking token loses more than about 14 to 15% against SOL |
| Borrow costs | Borrowing costs more than staking earns for a long time |
| Funding | Funding stays negative for a long time |
| Fast rallies | A very sharp rise in SOL puts pressure on the hedge |
| Beta custody | Vault rules are not yet locked on-chain |
| Solana | Network outages delay actions |
| Your access | You lose access to your email or Thaler wallet |

Past results do not promise future returns above the yield floor. This is not financial advice.
