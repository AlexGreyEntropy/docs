## What protects your deposit

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Hedge | SOL's price does not change your deposit's value |
| Your own vault | Other vaults cannot affect yours |
| Policy | The vault can only use approved markets |
| Risk engine | Acts automatically before a position gets unsafe |

## Not a guarantee yet

Thaler is building a public reserve on Solana to back deposits. It is not live yet. Until it is, your deposit is not guaranteed.

## Not covered

- A hack or failure at Kamino, Jupiter, a perpetual market or a staking provider.
- A staking token losing its value against SOL.
- Losing access to your own wallet.

See [Risk disclosure](/security/risk-disclosure).
