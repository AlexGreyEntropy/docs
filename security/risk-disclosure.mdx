Thaler reduces risk, but cannot remove all of it. This is not financial advice.

| Risk | What could happen |
|------|-------------------|
| Other protocols | Kamino, Jupiter, a perpetual market or a staking provider is hacked or fails |
| Staking tokens | A staking token loses value against SOL |
| Borrow costs | Borrowing costs more than staking earns for a long time |
| Funding | Funding stays negative for a long time |
| Fast rallies | A sharp SOL rally puts pressure on the hedge |
| Custody | During the beta, vaults use Privy wallets, not on-chain policies |
| Audit | The audit is not finished yet |
| Solana | Network outages delay actions |
| Your access | You lose access to your wallet |

## Not guaranteed

- Your deposit.
- Any minimum return.
- Past results.

The private beta is not open to residents of restricted countries.
