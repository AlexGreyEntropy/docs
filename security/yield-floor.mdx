description: "The return the strategy is designed to hold in any market."

## What it is

The strategy is designed to earn near **7 to 8 % a year**, whether the market goes up, down or sideways.

This is a target, not a guarantee.

## Where it comes from

Staking pays about 5 to 6 % a year. The lending loop raises that to 8 % or more. The hedge is extra on top.

## How it was tested

- Over 200,000 simulations.
- Live vaults on Solana mainnet since June 2026.

So far, live vaults have earned above the floor.

## What can push returns lower

- A hack or failure at a market the vault uses.
- Borrow costs staying above staking yield for a long time.
- Closing very soon after opening.
