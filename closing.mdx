## When

You can close your vault at any time. We recommend keeping it open for at least 4 weeks.

Opening and closing a vault both cost a little to set up and unwind. A vault closed in the first days may not earn enough to cover that.

If you took [Yield Upfront](/yield-upfront), you can close once it is repaid.

## What you get back

- Your deposit.
- Any yield not yet paid out, minus the 11 % service fee.
- 0.03 SOL, half of your creation reserve.

Everything is paid in SOL to your Thaler wallet.

## What happens when you close

The vault closes the hedge, unwinds the lending loop, turns the staking token back into SOL and sends it to you. You can follow each step in the app. It takes a few minutes.
