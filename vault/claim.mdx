## How yield builds up

Yield accrues inside the vault continuously, from four places:

| Source | How it shows up |
|--------|-----------------|
| Staking | The LST is worth more SOL over time |
| Lending loop | The collateral side of the loop grows as it compounds |
| Funding | Funding payments land in the hedge account |
| Downside capture | Short profits are realised in USDC and swapped to SOL |

Everything is counted in SOL. Funding and short profits are paid in USDC on the perpetual venue and swapped to SOL once they clear the venue's minimum.

## The 4-week cycle

Every 4 weeks, the vault settles the yield it has generated, takes the 11 % service fee from that yield, and then does one of two things.

<Term
  word="auto-compound"
  part="noun"
  phonetic="aw · toh · kom · pownd"
  example="I left auto-compound on and let my earnings do the work."
>
  A vault setting that adds your earnings back in every 4 weeks, so they start earning too. On by default. Switch it off to get paid out instead.
</Term>

<img src="/images/updates/auto-claim.png" alt="auto-claim, defined" className="rounded-xl" />

<Term
  word="auto-claim"
  part="noun"
  phonetic="aw · toh · klaym"
  example="With auto-claim enabled, yield is paid out without manual collection."
>
  A vault feature that automatically collects earned yield at regular intervals and pays it out to the holder, with no action required.
</Term>

| | Auto-compound | Auto-claim |
|-|---------------|------------|
| Default | On | Off |
| Every 4 weeks | Net yield goes back into the vault | Net yield is paid to your wallet |
| Effect | Your yield starts earning yield | Your deposit stays the same size |
| Action needed | None | None |

Switch auto-compound off to get paid out instead. If you take [Yield Upfront](/vault/yield-upfront), the vault runs on auto-repay until the claim is settled, and neither setting applies until then.

## Settlement

<Term
  word="settlement"
  part="noun"
  phonetic="set · ul · ment"
  example="The settlement landed in my wallet in one transaction."
>
  The moment funds move from a vault to your wallet. Settlement is full or nothing: either every step completes or none does.
</Term>

What you see is what you receive. A claim never leaves the vault half-settled.

## What a claim does not do

- It does not close the vault. The strategy keeps running.
- It does not touch your deposit. Only generated yield is claimed.
- It does not reset the [lock period](/vault/close#lock-period).

## Why every 4 weeks

Each claim is a set of on-chain transactions across several venues. A 4-week cycle keeps network and swap costs small compared with the yield being moved, and lets the hedge's USDC earnings build past the minimum needed to swap them to SOL.
