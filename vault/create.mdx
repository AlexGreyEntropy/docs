## Before you start

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Access | Thaler is in private beta. Request access at [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance) |
| SOL | At least the minimum of your chosen risk profile, plus a little for network fees |
| Wallet | A Solana wallet |

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance) and sign in. Nothing leaves your wallet until
    you approve a transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a risk profile">
    Choose one of the six Thaler One profiles, from Safe to YOLO. Each shows its target return
    and its deposit band. The profile is fixed for the life of the vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your deposit">
    Enter an amount in SOL inside the profile's deposit band.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what happens to your yield">
    Auto-compound is on by default and reinvests yield every 4 weeks. Switch it off to have
    yield auto-claimed to your wallet instead. You can also take
    [Yield Upfront](/vault/yield-upfront).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms and sign">
    Read the terms and policy agreement and sign the creation transaction. It pays the vault
    creation fee and funds the vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it build">
    Vault creation takes under a minute. Your dedicated worker then builds the staking, lending
    and hedge legs inside the vault's policy. The vault appears on your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deposit bands

<Term
  word="deposit band"
  part="noun"
  phonetic="dih · poz · it · band"
  example="The deposit band for Optimistic starts at 11 SOL."
>
  The minimum and maximum SOL a vault of a given risk profile accepts.
</Term>

| Risk profile | Minimum | Maximum |
|--------------|---------|---------|
| Safe | 6 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| Conservative | 6 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| Balanced | 8 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| Optimistic | 11 SOL | 1,112 SOL |
| Aggressive | 9 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| YOLO | 8 SOL | 1,111 SOL |

The minimums cover the operating minimums of the venues the vault uses. The first beta round used a fixed 2 SOL deposit to keep early risk small; that size no longer applies.

## Multiple vaults, one wallet

You can run several vaults side by side, for example a Safe vault and an Aggressive vault. Each is isolated, with its own risk profile, worker and policy.

## What you do not configure

| Decision | Made by |
|----------|---------|
| Staking token | Pulse Router, within the policy |
| Lending market | Pulse Router, within the policy |
| Perpetual venue | Pulse Router, within the policy |
| Rebalancing and safety actions | The risk engine |
| Stop-loss or take-profit | Not applicable, the strategy is rule-based |
