## Steps

1. Go to [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance) and sign in.
2. Pick a risk profile.
3. Enter how much SOL to deposit.
4. Sign the transaction.

Your vault is ready in under a minute. The worker then sets up staking, lending and the hedge for you.

## How much to deposit

| Profile | Minimum | Maximum |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Safe | 6 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| Conservative | 6 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| Balanced | 8 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| Optimistic | 11 SOL | 1,112 SOL |
| Aggressive | 9 SOL | 1,111 SOL |
| YOLO | 8 SOL | 1,111 SOL |

## More than one vault

You can open as many vaults as you like, each with its own risk profile.

## What Thaler handles for you

Choosing markets, rebalancing, compounding and paying out yield. You do not need to manage anything.
