## Fees

| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| Service fee | 11 % of your yield |
| Vault creation fee | A small amount of SOL, half refunded when you close |

That is all. No deposit fee, no management fee, and no closing penalty.

## Example

You deposit 10 SOL and your vault earns 1.3 SOL in a year.

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Yield | 1.300 SOL |
| Service fee (11 %) | 0.143 SOL |
| You keep | 1.157 SOL |

Returns shown in the app are already after the fee.

## Network costs

Solana transaction and swap costs are paid from the vault. These are not Thaler fees.
