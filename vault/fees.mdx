## Fee schedule

| Fee | Rate | When | Paid from |
|-----|------|------|-----------|
| Service fee | 11 % of generated yield | At each 4-week cycle and at close | Generated yield |
| Vault creation fee | Small fixed SOL amount, shown before you sign | At creation | Your wallet |
| Creation fee refund | 50 % of the creation fee comes back | At close | Paid to you |

<Term
  word="service fee"
  part="noun"
  phonetic="sur · vis · fee"
  example="The service fee only came out of my yield, never my deposit."
>
  The 11 % share of generated yield that Thaler keeps to run the engine. It is never taken from your deposit.
</Term>

If a vault generates no yield, the service fee is zero.

## Fees Thaler does not charge

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| Deposit fee | None |
| Management fee | None, the service fee is performance-based |
| Closing penalty | None, once the 4-week lock period has passed |

## Network and venue costs

The worker pays Solana network fees, swap fees and venue fees on your vault's behalf from the vault's balance. Swaps route through Titan with fees under 10 basis points. These are costs of using Solana and its venues, not Thaler fees.

## Net versus gross

Target returns and the current APR shown by Thaler are net of the service fee. You do not need to subtract anything.

```text
net_yield = gross_yield × (1 − 0.11)
```

## Worked example

A 10 SOL Balanced vault that generates 1.30 SOL of gross yield over a year, held with auto-claim on:

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Deposit | 10.000 SOL |
| Gross yield | 1.300 SOL |
| Service fee (11 %) | 0.143 SOL |
| Net yield, paid every 4 weeks | 1.157 SOL |
| Deposit returned at close | 10.000 SOL |
| Plus 50 % of the creation fee | Refunded at close |

The figures are illustrative. Actual yield depends on the market and the risk profile.
