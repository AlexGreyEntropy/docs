---
title: "Closing a vault"
description: "When you can close, and what you get back."
---

## When

Any time after the vault is **4 weeks** old. There is no penalty.

If you used [Yield Upfront](/vault/yield-upfront), you can close once it is paid back.

## What you get back

- Your deposit.
- Any yield not yet paid out, minus the 11 % fee.
- Half of your vault creation fee.

Everything is paid in SOL to your wallet, in one go.

## What happens behind the scenes

The vault closes the hedge, unwinds the lending loop, turns the staking token back into SOL, and sends it to you. Either every step completes or none does.
