## Every 4 weeks

Your vault runs in 4-week (28-day) <span className="term term-cycle" tabIndex={0}>cycles</span>. At the end of each cycle, Thaler collects your yield for you. This is called <span className="term term-auto-claim" tabIndex={0}>auto-claim</span>. You do not need to do anything.

The 11% <span className="term term-service-fee" tabIndex={0}>service fee</span> comes out of the yield at this point. Your deposit is never touched.

## Then you choose

| Auto-compound | What happens to your yield |
|---------------|----------------------------|
| On (default) | Goes back into your vault, so it earns too |
| Off | Goes to your Thaler wallet |

Switch <span className="term term-auto-compound" tabIndex={0}>auto-compound</span> on or off at any time, for each vault.

## Claim now

Coming in the next upgrade: a **Claim now** button, to collect your yield before the 4-week cycle ends.

- It costs an extra 1% of the yield you claim, on top of the 11% service fee. So you pay 12% on that claim.
- The extra 1% goes straight into the [insurance fund](/safety#insurance-fund-and-treasury).
- If you can wait for auto-claim, you pay only the 11%.

For comparison, most apps charge for fast access too. Swaps usually cost 0.5% to 1%, and some staked stablecoins make you wait 1 to 7 days to withdraw.

## Good to know

- Your yield is paid in SOL.
- Claiming does not close your vault.
- If you took [Yield Upfront](/yield-upfront), your yield pays that back first.
- Each time SOL drops, your vault takes hedge profits in USDC and swaps them to SOL. This happens on its own and counts toward your yield.
