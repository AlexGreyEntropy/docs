## What is Thaler

Thaler is a <span className="term term-yield" tabIndex={0}>yield</span> app on Solana. You put <span className="term term-sol" tabIndex={0}>SOL</span> into your own <span className="term term-vault" tabIndex={0}>vault</span>, and the vault earns more SOL for you. It earns whether the price of SOL goes up or down.

Our first strategy is called **<span className="term term-thaler-one" tabIndex={0}>Thaler One</span>**.

## Why it is different

Most ways to earn on SOL only work well when the price holds or rises. When SOL falls, you lose more on the price than you earn in yield.

Thaler adds a <span className="term term-hedge" tabIndex={0}>hedge</span> that cancels out SOL's price moves on your deposit. So your return comes from the yield, not from guessing where the price goes next. When SOL falls, the hedge actually earns more. Being hedged like this is called being <span className="term term-delta-neutral" tabIndex={0}>delta-neutral</span>.
