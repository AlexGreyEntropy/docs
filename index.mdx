## What is Thaler

Thaler is a yield protocol on Solana. You deposit SOL into your own vault. The vault earns yield whether SOL goes up or down, and pays it in SOL.

## How it works

Each vault runs three things at once:

1. **Staking.** Your SOL is staked and earns staking rewards.
2. **Lending.** The staked SOL is looped on a lending market to earn more.
3. **Hedge.** A short position cancels out SOL's price moves, and earns when SOL falls.
