## Abstract

Thaler is a Solana protocol that turns a SOL deposit into a delta-neutral yield position. Each vault runs three coordinated legs: a liquid staking position, a lending loop, and a short perpetual hedge sized against your deposit. Because the hedge cancels the price exposure of your principal, the vault earns from rates and market movement rather than from SOL going up. Yield is measured and paid in SOL.

Every vault is isolated. It holds only your deposit, runs only the strategy and risk profile you picked, and can only perform the actions its policy allows.

This documentation is for depositors, integrators and reviewers. It does not publish strategy parameters, position sizes or operational thresholds.

## Position summary

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Deposit asset | SOL |
| Risk profiles | Six, from Safe to YOLO |
| Deposit size | Per profile, from 6 SOL to 1,111 SOL |
| Yield sources | Liquid staking, lending loop, perpetual hedge |
| Target return | 8.2 % to 20 % a year, depending on profile |
| Structural floor | Near 7 to 8 % a year (design target, not a guarantee) |
| Yield handling | Auto-compound every 4 weeks (default), or auto-claim to your wallet |
| Yield Upfront | Claim up to 6 months of expected net yield on day one |
| Lock period | 4 weeks from creation, no closing penalty after that |
| Service fee | 11 % of generated yield only |
| Custody (private beta) | Isolated Privy agentic wallet per vault, scoped authorization key |
| Custody (after audit) | Squads smart account with immutable on-chain policies |

## Private beta

<Note>
  Thaler is in private beta on Solana mainnet. Access is by invitation. Request it at
  [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance). There is no Thaler token, no presale and no airdrop.
  If you see one, it is not us.
</Note>

The beta runs on the same risk engine that is going to audit. The main change after the audit is custody: vaults move from Privy agentic wallets to Squads smart accounts with immutable on-chain policies. See [Custody and policy](/overview/custody-and-policy).
