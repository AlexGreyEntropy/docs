### What is Thaler?

A yield protocol on Solana. You deposit SOL into your own vault, and it earns whether SOL goes up or down.

### Is there a Thaler token?

No.

### What can I deposit?

SOL.

### How much can I earn?

The target is 8.2 % to 20 % a year, depending on your risk profile. This is not a promise.

### Is my money pooled with others?

No. Every vault is separate.

### Can Thaler take my funds?

No. Payouts can only go to your wallet.

### When do I get my yield?

Every 4 weeks. By default it is added back to your vault. Switch off auto-compound to get it paid out instead.

### What is Yield Upfront?

Get up to 6 months of expected yield on day one. Your vault pays it back over time.

### How much do I need?

6 to 11 SOL minimum, depending on the risk profile.

### When can I close my vault?

After 4 weeks, with no penalty.

### What are the fees?

11 % of your yield, plus a small creation fee that is half refunded when you close.

### Is my deposit guaranteed?

Not yet. See [Principal safety](/security/principal-protection).

### How do I get access?

Request it at [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance).
