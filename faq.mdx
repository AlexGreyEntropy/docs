## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Thaler?" defaultOpen>
    A non-custodial yield protocol on Solana. You deposit SOL into your own vault, which runs a
    delta-neutral strategy across liquid staking, a lending loop and a short perpetual hedge.
    It earns from market movement, not price direction. See
    [What is Thaler](/overview/what-is-thaler).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a Thaler token?">
    No. There is no token, no presale and no airdrop. If you see one, it is not us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Thaler now?">
    Thaler is in private beta on Solana mainnet. Request access at
    [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can I deposit?">
    SOL. Yield is measured and paid in SOL too.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Custody

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Thaler custodial?">
    No. Each vault is its own isolated wallet. During the private beta it is a Privy agentic
    wallet that the engine operates through a scoped authorization key, never your private key.
    After the audit, vaults move to Squads smart accounts with immutable on-chain policies. See
    [Custody and policy](/overview/custody-and-policy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are deposits pooled?">
    No. Every vault holds only its owner's funds. If one vault were ever exploited, every other
    vault would be unaffected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to manage anything?">
    No. Your dedicated worker rebalances, compounds, claims and hedges inside the vault's
    policy. You sign to create a vault, to take Yield Upfront, and to close.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Yield

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much can I earn?">
    The strategy targets 8.2 % to 20 % a year, net of fees, depending on the risk profile. As
    of 13 November 2026, the current APR across live vaults was 15.45 %. These are not promises.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when SOL falls?">
    The short hedge books the fall as profit, which is swapped to SOL. Falling markets are when
    the vault tends to earn most.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when SOL rises?">
    The lending loop keeps compounding and funding usually pays the short. Your yield, already
    in SOL, is worth more in dollars.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a guaranteed minimum?">
    Not yet. The strategy is designed around a structural floor near 7 to 8 % a year, but a
    guarantee needs the on-chain reserve, which is still being designed. See
    [Structural floor](/security/yield-floor).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get my yield?">
    Every 4 weeks. Auto-compound is on by default and adds yield back into the vault. Switch it
    off and yield is auto-claimed to your wallet instead. See [Yield and claims](/vault/claim).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Yield Upfront?">
    Claim up to 6 months of expected net yield on the day you open a vault. The vault locks,
    runs on auto-repay, and its own yield settles the claim, typically in about 4 to 5 months.
    See [Yield Upfront](/vault/yield-upfront).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Risk profiles and deposits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the risk profiles?">
    Six: Safe, Conservative, Balanced, Optimistic, Aggressive and YOLO. The profile sets how
    hard the strategy leans on each leg and is fixed for the life of the vault.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much do I need to deposit?">
    It depends on the profile, from 6 SOL (Safe, Conservative) up to 11 SOL (Optimistic), with a
    maximum of 1,111 SOL. See [Creating a vault](/vault/create#deposit-bands).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I run more than one vault?">
    Yes. You can run several vaults side by side, each with its own profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Closing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When can I close my vault?">
    After 4 weeks. There is no closing penalty after that. If you took Yield Upfront, the vault
    stays locked until the claim is settled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I get back?">
    Your deposit plus unclaimed yield, minus the 11 % service fee on that yield, plus half of
    your vault creation fee, all in SOL. See [Closing a vault](/vault/close).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does Thaler charge?">
    An 11 % service fee on generated yield, and a small vault creation fee, half of which is
    refunded at close. No deposit fee, management fee or closing penalty. See [Fees](/vault/fees).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the returns shown gross or net?">
    Net of the service fee.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Strategy details

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which protocols does a vault use?">
    Nine liquid staking tokens, Kamino and Jupiter Lend for lending, and Pacifica, Phoenix and
    Bulk (in testing) for the hedge. Pulse Router picks the best one for each leg.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are exact parameters private?">
    Leverage values, rebalance thresholds and venue weights would let others front-run
    rebalances. The legs, venues and rules are public; the calibration is not.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
