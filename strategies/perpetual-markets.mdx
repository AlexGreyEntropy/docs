## What it does

The vault opens a short SOL position the same size as your deposit. When SOL's price moves, the hedge moves the other way, so your deposit keeps its value in SOL.

## How it earns

| Source | When |
|--------|------|
| Funding | When long traders pay short traders, which is most of the time |
| Falling prices | Every time SOL falls, the hedge takes profit and resets |

Profit is paid in USDC and swapped back to SOL. This is why Thaler earns most when other yield earns least.

## When SOL rises

The hedge uses SOL as collateral, so its collateral grows as SOL rises. If it needs more, the risk engine adds SOL from the lending loop. The hedge has held through rallies from $65 to $84 and from $74 to $110.
