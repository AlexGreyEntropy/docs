## Three parts

| Part | What it earns |
|------|---------------|
| [Staking](/strategies/liquid-staking) | Solana staking rewards |
| [Lending](/strategies/lending-markets) | Extra yield from looping staked SOL |
| [Hedge](/strategies/perpetual-markets) | Funding payments, and profit when SOL falls |

## How they fit together

1. Your SOL is staked into a staking token.
2. The staking token is looped on a lending market to earn more.
3. A short position is opened against your deposit, so SOL's price does not change its value.

When SOL falls, the hedge takes profit and resets at the lower price. When SOL rises, the lending loop keeps earning and the hedge usually earns funding.

## Risk profiles

You pick one when you create a vault. It cannot be changed later.

| Profile | Deposit |
|---------|---------|
| Safe | 6 to 1,111 SOL |
| Conservative | 6 to 1,111 SOL |
| Balanced | 8 to 1,111 SOL |
| Optimistic | 11 to 1,112 SOL |
| Aggressive | 9 to 1,111 SOL |
| YOLO | 8 to 1,111 SOL |

Higher profiles aim for higher returns and take more risk. Across all profiles, the target is **8.2 % to 20 % a year**, after fees.

## Structural floor

The strategy is designed to earn near **7 to 8 % a year** in any market. This is a target, not a guarantee. See [Structural floor](/security/yield-floor).
