## Three legs

A Thaler vault runs three legs at once. Each earns from a different source, and the conditions that slow one down tend to speed another up.

| Leg | Source of return | Page |
|-----|------------------|------|
| Liquid staking | Solana staking rewards, paid into the LST exchange rate | [Liquid staking](/strategies/liquid-staking) |
| Lending loop | LST yield minus the SOL borrow rate, compounding | [Lending markets](/strategies/lending-markets) |
| Perpetual hedge | Funding paid by longs, plus profit captured when SOL falls | [Perpetual markets](/strategies/perpetual-markets) |

<Note>
  Liquidity provision was part of the strategy in Round 1 of the private beta. It was removed
  from Round 2 onwards.
</Note>

## How the legs work together

1. Your SOL is staked into a liquid staking token.
2. The LST is supplied to a lending loop that borrows SOL against it and stakes again, earning the spread.
3. A small extra borrow funds a short SOL perpetual, sized against your deposit, with SOL as its collateral.

The short cancels the price exposure of your principal. When SOL falls, the short is closed to book the profit, the profit is swapped from USDC to SOL, and the short is reopened at the lower price. When SOL rises, the lending loop keeps compounding and funding usually flows to the short, while extra SOL from the loop's headroom is added as margin if the short needs it.

| Market | Lending loop | Perpetual hedge | Overall |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|---------|
| Calm or sideways | Earns steadily | Earns little | Slower return |
| Rising | Earns and compounds | Earns funding, holds under pressure | Steady return |
| Falling | Earns | Captures the move as profit | Highest return |
| Volatile | Earns | Captures dips and pullbacks | Higher return |

## Risk profiles

<Term
  word="risk profile"
  part="noun"
  phonetic="risk · proh · fyle"
  example="I picked the Safe risk profile for my first vault."
>
  The setting you choose at creation, from Safe to YOLO, that decides how hard the strategy leans on each yield source. It is fixed for the life of the vault.
</Term>

| Risk profile | Deposit band | Character |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| Safe | 6 to 1,111 SOL | Lowest leverage, widest safety margins |
| Conservative | 6 to 1,111 SOL | A small step toward the lending loop |
| Balanced | 8 to 1,111 SOL | Even weight between loop carry and hedge income |
| Optimistic | 11 to 1,112 SOL | More weight on the hedge |
| Aggressive | 9 to 1,111 SOL | Higher leverage, hedge income dominates |
| YOLO | 8 to 1,111 SOL | Highest leverage, most variable returns |

Across profiles, the strategy targets about **8.2 % to 20 % a year**, net of the service fee, with higher profiles at the top of that range and with a thinner margin for error. For reference, Safe targets around 11 %, Balanced around 13 % and Aggressive around 16 %.

The minimums are not arbitrary. They clear the operating minimums of the underlying protocols. For example, short profits on Pacifica settle in USDC and need at least $10 to swap back to SOL.

## Structural floor

The steadiest parts of the strategy are designed to hold a return near **7 to 8 % a year** whether the next months are bullish or bearish. Most of that comes from staking and the lending loop. See [Structural floor](/security/yield-floor) for what this is and what it is not.

## What is the same in every profile

- An isolated vault and a dedicated worker.
- Auto-compound or auto-claim every 4 weeks.
- Access to Yield Upfront.
- A 4-week lock period and no closing penalty after it.
- The 11 % service fee on generated yield only.

## What stays private

Exact leverage values, rebalance thresholds and venue weights are not published. Publishing them would let others front-run rebalances. The legs, venues and rules are public; the calibration is not.
