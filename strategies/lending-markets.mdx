## What it does

Staking alone pays about 5 to 6 % a year. The lending loop raises that.

1. The vault lends its staking token.
2. It borrows SOL against it.
3. It stakes that SOL again.

The vault earns staking yield on a bigger position and pays a smaller borrow cost. Earnings are added back in, so the loop compounds.

## Why it is low risk

Both sides of the loop are SOL, so SOL's price moving does not push the loan toward liquidation.

## When rates move

If borrow costs spike, the risk engine reduces borrowing automatically. In July 2026, during a record spike on Kamino, this cut the extra cost by about 87 %.
