## The short version

Your vault does three things at once:

1. **Stakes** your SOL so it earns <span className="term term-staking" tabIndex={0}>staking</span> rewards.
2. **Loops** it on a <span className="term term-lending-market" tabIndex={0}>lending market</span> to earn more.
3. **Hedges** it, so SOL's price does not change the value of your deposit.

You do not manage any of this. The vault runs on its own, day and night.

## 1. Staking

Your SOL is turned into a <span className="term term-liquid-staking-token" tabIndex={0}>liquid staking token</span>. It earns staking rewards and can still be used by the rest of the strategy.

Supported tokens: jitoSOL, mSOL, jupSOL, INF, bSOL, stkeSOL, hSOL, pSOL and lanternSOL.

## 2. Lending loop

The staking token goes into a <span className="term term-lending-loop" tabIndex={0}>lending loop</span>:

1. The vault lends the staking token.
2. It <span className="term term-borrow" tabIndex={0}>borrows</span> SOL against it.
3. It stakes that SOL too.

Now the vault earns staking rewards on a bigger amount. Both sides are SOL, so a change in SOL's price does not push the loan toward <span className="term term-liquidation" tabIndex={0}>liquidation</span>.

Supported lending markets: Kamino and Jupiter Lend.

## 3. Hedge

The vault opens a <span className="term term-short" tabIndex={0}>short</span> on a <span className="term term-perpetual-market" tabIndex={0}>perpetual market</span>, the same size as your deposit. When SOL's price moves, the short moves the other way. Your deposit keeps its value.

The short earns in two ways:

- **<span className="term term-funding-rate" tabIndex={0}>Funding</span>.** Most of the time, traders betting on SOL going up pay the short side.
- **Falling prices.** Each time SOL drops, the vault takes a small profit from the short, called a <span className="term term-scalp" tabIndex={0}>scalp</span>, and resets it at the lower price.

Supported perpetual markets: Pacifica, Phoenix and Bulk.

## How it earns in each market

| Market | What happens | Rough return |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| SOL goes up | The lending loop earns more and funding grows. The short loses, but the SOL <span className="term term-collateral" tabIndex={0}>collateral</span> backing it gains just as much, so it evens out. | About 12 to 13 % a year |
| SOL goes down | The lending loop and funding earn a bit less. The short takes profit on every drop, and that profit buys back SOL at a lower price (<span className="term term-dca" tabIndex={0}>DCA</span>). | 20 % a year or more |

These are rough guides, not promises. Returns change with the market.

## Your return is counted in SOL

Your deposit and your yield are both in SOL. If SOL's price rises, your SOL is also worth more in dollars.

## Who makes the choices

**<span className="term term-pulse-router" tabIndex={0}>Pulse Router</span>** picks the best staking token, lending market and perpetual market for your vault, based on what pays the most right now.

**The <span className="term term-risk-engine" tabIndex={0}>risk engine</span>** watches every position and acts on its own:

| When | It |
|------|----|
| Borrowing gets expensive | Borrows less |
| SOL rises fast | Adds more SOL to back the short |
| SOL falls | Takes profit on the short and resets it |
| A position drifts | Rebalances it |

Hedge profits are paid in <span className="term term-usdc" tabIndex={0}>USDC</span>. The vault <span className="term term-swap" tabIndex={0}>swaps</span> them back to SOL through Titan Exchange.
