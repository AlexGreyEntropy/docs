## Before you start

You need access to the private beta. Request it at [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance). You can also ask for a short onboarding call, and someone from the team will set it up with you.

## Steps

1. **Sign in** at [app.thaler.finance](https://app.thaler.finance) with your email. Social sign-in is coming soon.
2. **Add SOL to your <span className="term term-thaler-wallet" tabIndex={0}>Thaler wallet</span>.** It is created for you when you sign in. Send SOL to its address from any Solana <span className="term term-wallet" tabIndex={0}>wallet</span> or exchange.
3. **Pick a <span className="term term-risk-profile" tabIndex={0}>risk profile</span>.**
4. **Enter how much SOL** to put in.
5. **Accept the terms and open the vault.** The terms include the [early close penalty](/closing).

Your vault is ready in under 30 seconds. It then stakes, loops and hedges your SOL on its own.

Send a little more than your deposit. Opening a vault needs an extra 0.06 SOL <span className="term term-creation-reserve" tabIndex={0}>creation reserve</span>. Half of it comes back when you close.

## Pick a risk profile

| Profile | Deposit | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Safe | 6 to 1,111 SOL | You want the calmest option |
| Conservative | 6 to 1,111 SOL | You want a little more yield, still with care |
| Balanced | 8 to 1,111 SOL | You are fine with more risk for more yield |

The profiles differ in how much <span className="term term-leverage" tabIndex={0}>leverage</span> the vault uses on the lending loop and the hedge. More leverage can earn more, but leaves less room for error.

You pick the profile once. It cannot be changed later. To try another profile, open another vault.

## Why there is a minimum and a maximum

**Minimum 6 SOL.** Hedge profits are paid in USDC and swapped back to SOL. A swap only happens once there is at least 10 USDC to swap. With 2 SOL, each hedge profit was about 1.50 USDC, so the vault had to wait for 8 or 9 profits before it could swap once. With 6 SOL, each profit lands close to 10 USDC, so it swaps back to SOL much sooner.

**Maximum 1,111 SOL per vault.** Some perpetual markets limit how much can be taken out at once. Pacifica allows 50,000 USDC every 24 hours, and Phoenix pays out in a queue of 400 USDC parts. Bullet has no limit. Capping each vault keeps closing it simple and safe within these limits.

## More than one vault

Open as many vaults as you like, each with its own profile.

To deposit more than 1,111 SOL, open several vaults. You can also open them at different times, for example a few during a rally.

## Adding more SOL

Adding more SOL to an existing vault is coming soon. Taking out part of your deposit is not planned. To get your SOL back, [close the vault](/closing).

## In the app

Once your vault is open, the app shows:

- How much you deposited and earned.
- Your vault's performance and return over time.
- Where your SOL is: staking, lending and hedge.
- When the next 4-week <span className="term term-cycle" tabIndex={0}>cycle</span> ends.
- Activity, such as yield collected and hedge profits taken.

You can also create a share card of your earnings and post it on X.

## Your Thaler wallet

Your Thaler wallet holds your SOL before it goes into a vault, and receives your yield and your SOL when you close. You can send SOL from it to any other Solana wallet, or export its <span className="term term-private-key" tabIndex={0}>private key</span> to use it in another wallet app.
