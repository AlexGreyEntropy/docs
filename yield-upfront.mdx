## What it is

<span className="term term-yield-upfront" tabIndex={0}>Yield Upfront</span> pays you some of your vault's expected yield today, instead of waiting for it. Your deposit keeps earning as usual, and the vault pays the amount back over time.

## How much you can get

| Profile | Up to |
|---------|-------|
| Safe | 6 months of expected yield |
| Conservative | 5.5 months |
| Balanced | 5 months |

You can take less than the maximum. The app shows the exact amount for your vault.

## How it works

1. Open **Get yield upfront** on your vault. You can do this when you open the vault or later.
2. Choose the amount, read the terms and sign.
3. The SOL lands in your Thaler wallet.
4. Your vault goes into <span className="term term-auto-repay" tabIndex={0}>auto-repay</span>. Its yield pays back the amount until it is repaid.

Once it is repaid, you can take Yield Upfront again after a one-hour wait.

## What it costs

- The SOL is borrowed on your vault's lending loop, so it pays that market's borrow interest.
- The 11 % service fee is added to the amount up front.

## Good to know

- Until it is repaid, all your yield goes to paying it back. None is paid out to you.
- You cannot close the vault until it is repaid.
- If the market is slow, paying back takes longer. If the market is busy, it is faster.
- If your yield does not cover it, the rest comes out of your deposit when the vault closes.
