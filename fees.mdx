## Fees

| Fee | Amount | When |
|-----|--------|------|
| <span className="term term-service-fee" tabIndex={0}>Service fee</span> | 11% of your yield | Every 4 weeks, and on any yield paid out when you close |
| <span className="term term-creation-reserve" tabIndex={0}>Creation reserve</span> | 0.06 SOL | When you open a vault. 0.03 SOL comes back when you close |
| Early close penalty | 3% of your deposit plus yield after the service fee, falling to 0% over your first 4 weeks | Only if you close before your first auto-claim. See [Close a Vault](/closing) |
| Claim now (next upgrade) | Extra 1% of the yield you claim | Only if you claim before the cycle ends. Goes to the insurance fund |

There is no deposit fee and no management fee. The 11% service fee applies to all yield your vault earns, always. It only comes out of yield, never your deposit. The early close penalty is worked out after it.

Fees go to the Thaler treasury and the insurance fund. See [Safety and Risks](/safety#insurance-fund-and-treasury).

The 0.03 SOL that is kept covers your vault's setup and its network fees. You do not pay network fees yourself.

## Example

You deposit 10 SOL and your vault earns 1.3 SOL in a year.

| Item | SOL |
|------|-----|
| Yield | 1.300 |
| Service fee (11%) | 0.143 |
| You keep | 1.157 |

Returns shown in the app are already after the fee.

## Yield Upfront

If you take [Yield Upfront](/yield-upfront), the 11% fee is added to the amount when you take it, and the borrowed SOL pays the lending market's borrow interest until it is repaid.
